06 — GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Is MindBridge a therapy app? ＋

No. MindBridge is being designed to make mental health information easier to understand and professional support easier to find. It does not provide therapy or replace a qualified professional.

Can MindBridge diagnose depression or anxiety? ＋

No. It will not diagnose conditions, prescribe medication, or provide treatment instructions. A qualified health professional is the right person to assess personal health concerns.

Will it be available in Hausa? ＋

That's part of the plan. English and Hausa are intended to be supported, with translations reviewed for clarity and cultural context before release.

Can I use it without an internet connection? ＋

Selected educational content is planned to work offline. The exact offline experience is still being developed and tested.

Is the app available to download now? ＋

Not yet. MindBridge is in development. We'll publish official download links here when a tested release is ready. Please be cautious of unofficial downloads.
