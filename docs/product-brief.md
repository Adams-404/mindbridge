# Product brief

## Product
MindBridge AI is a mobile-first mental-health information and referral platform for underserved Nigerian communities, with English and Hausa as first-class languages.

## Problem
The supplied MindBridge Supporting Evidence Document identifies limited awareness of depression/anxiety, stigma, difficulty finding trustworthy information, limited access to qualified professionals, financial/geographical barriers, language and digital-literacy challenges, and poor referral follow-up.

## Audiences
Young people and students; women and families; people experiencing emotional distress; rural and underserved populations; Hausa-speaking communities; caregivers and community members.

## Core journeys
- Choose English or Hausa and understand the product's scope.
- Explore reviewed education: depression, anxiety, everyday stress, emotional wellbeing, and when to seek professional help.
- Optionally record a simple mood reflection. It is skippable and non-diagnostic.
- Try guided breathing/relaxation with pause and stop controls.
- Find verified providers and understand referrals once a real verification process exists.
- Manage personal follow-up reminders.
- Future: AI-assisted general education, only after safety review and backend discussion.

## Scope now
Build the responsive public landing page, then a functional Flutter frontend with local/demo content, localisation-ready structure, accessibility, tests, and repository interfaces.

## Explicitly out of scope
No backend, accounts, cloud sync, live AI, diagnosis, treatment/medication recommendations, crisis prediction, clinical efficacy claims, real provider claims, or collection of real sensitive health information.

## Prototype acceptance
A user understands what MindBridge does/does not do; can navigate without an account; can change language; can skip check-in; can read bundled learning content; can use a breathing exercise; and sees honest empty/demo states rather than fake live services.

## Open questions
Validate Hausa terminology; decide if check-ins should ever persist; verify local crisis pathways; define provider verification ownership; confirm age/safeguarding policy; consult clinicians and intended users.
