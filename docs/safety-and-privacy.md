# Safety, privacy and responsible AI

## Boundary
MindBridge is an educational and referral-support concept. It is not a diagnostic tool, therapist, emergency service, or substitute for qualified care. No diagnosis, prognosis, medication advice, treatment instructions, risk prediction, or authoritative AI responses in this prototype.

## Safety requirements
1. Distinguish general education from individual clinical advice.
2. Encourage professional support when concerns persist, worsen, or affect daily life.
3. Only publish urgent support contacts/pathways after local verification.
4. Never imply the app is monitored or can dispatch help.
5. Breathing/grounding is not a crisis response; let users stop if uncomfortable.
6. Check-ins do not create clinical labels, scores, or automated escalation.
7. Every health article needs a qualified reviewer, sources, review date, and version.
8. Provider listings need verification, last-checked date, and correction/removal process.
9. Complete clinical risk and safeguarding review before user pilots.

## Crisis pathways
Do not invent a universal Nigerian emergency number or assume services are available in every state. Consult local clinicians and emergency organisations; verify availability, geography, hours, language, cost, and escalation steps. Display verification dates and limitations. Until verified, use a general safety notice without claiming emergency response.

## Privacy baseline
No account required; no names, phone numbers, contacts, precise location, or health history collected. Do not persist mood entries in this milestone. No analytics, advertising SDKs, trackers, or third-party AI calls. Future storage requires purpose limitation, minimisation, retention/deletion rules, encryption, threat modelling, consent, and clear data-flow explanations. Never send sensitive check-in text to analytics/crash reports.

## Future AI gate
Define narrow educational use cases and exclusions; ground responses in clinician-reviewed sources; evaluate hallucinations, unsafe advice, bias, Hausa quality and crisis prompts; provide human-support routes; define consent/retention; establish monitoring, incident response and rollback. Never market it as a therapist or diagnostic assistant.

This is a product baseline, not a clinical safety case or legal opinion. Obtain professional clinical, privacy, security and Nigerian regulatory review before real-world testing or deployment.
