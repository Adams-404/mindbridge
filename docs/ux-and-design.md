# UX and visual direction

## Brand
Warm, grounded, contemporary, human. Not a hospital portal and not a generic AI dashboard.

## Visual system
Warm milk/soft ivory canvas; deep evergreen primary; restrained clay/terracotta accent; charcoal text; milk-white surfaces; thin outlines, modest radii, occasional hard offset shadows. Neo-brutalist influence through confident typography, editorial grids, a few bold borders and deliberate asymmetry. Avoid loud color blocks, gradients, glassmorphism, and excessive pills.

## Interaction
One clear primary action per screen. Plain language. No streaks, leaderboards, badges, or pressure-based engagement. Check-ins are optional and skippable. Do not use color alone for mood or urgency. Breathing has pause/stop controls. Predictable navigation, visible back actions, no forced registration or unnecessary permissions.

## Navigation
Bottom navigation: Home, Learn, Check-in, Support. Settings reachable from home.

## Landing page
Navigation; hero with honest product status and app preview; core capabilities; how it works; safety/privacy; English/Hausa and low-bandwidth commitments; FAQ; closing CTA; footer/disclaimer.

## Tone
Warm, direct, respectful, non-judgmental. Avoid promises like "feel better instantly", "beat depression", "AI therapist", or "always here for you".

## Accessibility
Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA on web; dynamic text and screen-reader support in app; generous touch targets; visible focus; keyboard-accessible navigation/FAQ; reduced-motion support; no autoplay; responsive reflow; complete reviewed translations.
